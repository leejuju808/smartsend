# Julian Lee - Founder, AUREV Labs

## Bio

Julian Lee is the founder of AUREV Labs and the creator of AUREV OS, the AI Operating System for business builders. Prior to launching AUREV Labs, Julian built SmartSend AI into a leading platform for AI-powered cold email automation, serving thousands of SMBs and founders.

Julian is passionate about using AI to eliminate the complexity that prevents small businesses from scaling. AUREV OS represents his vision of a unified platform that brings together outreach, operations, and automation—tools that were previously fragmented across multiple vendors.
