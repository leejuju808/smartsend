# Storyboard Frame 01: THE SPARK

## Visual Composition
- **Background**: Pure black (#0A0A0A)
- **Foreground**: Single gold pulse (#F8C948) in center of frame
- **Camera**: Static, centered, extreme close-up transitioning to wide pull-back
- **Lighting**: Minimal ambient, pulse provides all illumination

## Animation Sequence
1. **0:00-0:03**: Black screen, single gold pulse begins as faint heartbeat
   - Pulse grows from 5% opacity to 30% opacity
   - Rhythmic pulsing at 60 BPM
   
2. **0:03-0:06**: Pulse intensifies, begins to morph
   - Gold particles emerge from pulse center
   - Particles form abstract shapes, suggest electricity/lightning
   
3. **0:06-0:10**: Particles coalesce into AUREV ⚡ symbol
   - Symbol materializes from particle cloud
   - Final form glows at 80% opacity
   - Subtle rotation (15° clockwise)

## Typography
- Text: None in this frame (voice-over only)

## Color Palette
- Primary: #0A0A0A (black)
- Accent: #F8C948 (gold)
- Pulse glow: #F8C948 at varying opacity (5-80%)

## Sound Design Notes
- Low-frequency hum (40 Hz) underlying heartbeat pulse
- Ambient synth pad building from silence
- No dialogue during visual sequence

## Duration
10 seconds
