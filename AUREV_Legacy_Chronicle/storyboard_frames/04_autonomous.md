# Storyboard Frame 04: AUTONOMOUS NETWORK (2028)

## Visual Composition
- **Layout**: Full-screen world map view
- **Background**: Deep space black with subtle starfield
- **Foreground**: Earth (stylized, low-poly aesthetic)
- **Camera**: Slow zoom from space to Earth surface, then pull back to reveal network

## Animation Sequence
1. **0:44-0:50**: Earth rotates into view from blackness
   - Earth is stylized: dark continents, blue oceans
   - Lighting: Rim-lit with gold glow
   - Camera angle: 45° from equatorial plane

2. **0:50-0:58**: Thousands of nodes illuminate across Earth
   - Nodes appear as golden pinpoints
   - Sequential activation: starts from single region, spreads globally
   - Each node pulses with golden light
   - Connection lines form between nearby nodes
   - Network density increases exponentially

3. **0:58-1:04**: Satellite constellations form lightning motif
   - Satellites orbit Earth in geometric patterns
   - Orbits align to form ⚡ lightning bolt shape
   - Satellites pulse in synchronized rhythm
   - Constellations emit golden trails

4. **1:04-1:06**: Network visualization overlay
   - Network graph superimposes on Earth
   - Data flows visible as golden streams
   - Scale indicator appears: "100,000 organizations"

## Typography
- **Stat Overlay**: "100,000 organizations connected"
- Font: Poppins Bold
- Color: #F8C948
- Position: Upper right
- Animation: Count-up animation (0 → 100,000)

## Color Palette
- Primary: #0A0A0A (space background)
- Earth: #1A2A3A (dark blue-gray continents), #2A3A4A (oceans)
- Nodes: #F8C948 (gold, full brightness)
- Connection lines: #F8C948 at 40% opacity
- Satellite trails: #F8C948 with motion blur

## Sound Design Notes
- Deep space ambience (low-frequency rumble)
- Node activation: Digital ping (rising pitch)
- Network growth: Layered orchestral strings
- Satellite orbits: Mechanical whir with metallic resonance

## Duration
22 seconds
