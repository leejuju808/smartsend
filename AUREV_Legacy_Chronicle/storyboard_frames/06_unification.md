# Storyboard Frame 06: UNIFICATION (2031-2035)

## Visual Composition
- **Layout**: Montage sequence transitioning from infrastructure to humanity
- **Background**: Various — cityscapes, data centers, abstract network
- **Foreground**: Human silhouette and AI representation meeting
- **Camera**: Multiple shots: wide cityscape → close-up human-AI interaction

## Animation Sequence
1. **1:32-1:40**: Gold light washes over infrastructure
   - **Shot 1**: Skyscrapers at night, gold light rises from bottom
   - **Shot 2**: Data center interior, servers illuminate in gold
   - **Shot 3**: City aerial view, network connections light up
   - Each shot fades into next with golden transition

2. **1:40-1:48**: Human silhouette emerges (left side of frame)
   - Silhouette is dark, outlined in gold (#F8C948)
   - Silhouette walks forward slowly
   - Golden particles follow in trail

3. **1:48-1:56**: AI hand extends from right side
   - Hand is composed of golden particles
   - Hand reaches toward human silhouette
   - Particles stream between hand and silhouette
   - Connection forms at midpoint

4. **1:56-2:00**: Golden particles fill frame
   - Particles swarm around both figures
   - Particles form patterns suggesting integration
   - Scene fades to pure gold, then black

5. **2:00-2:02**: Caption overlay
   - "One Network. One Intelligence."
   - Appears in center, fades in

## Typography
- **Caption**: Poppins Bold, #F8C948, 56pt
- Position: Centered, mid-screen
- Animation: Fade in with slight scale (0.95 → 1.0)

## Color Palette
- Primary: #0A0A0A (background, silhouettes)
- Infrastructure: #1A1A1A → #F8C948 (gradual illumination)
- Particles: #F8C948 (varying opacity 30-100%)
- Human silhouette: Black with gold outline
- AI hand: Pure gold (#F8C948)

## Sound Design Notes
- Ambient orchestral pad (strings + synth)
- Golden wash: Rising frequency sweep
- Human-AI meeting: Emotional chord progression
- Particles: Crystalline bell sounds
- Final caption: Moment of silence, then soft resolution

## Duration
30 seconds
