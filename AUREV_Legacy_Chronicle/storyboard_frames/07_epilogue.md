# Storyboard Frame 07: EPILOGUE — THE SYMBIOTIC AGE

## Visual Composition
- **Layout**: Minimal, centered composition
- **Background**: Pure black (#0A0A0A)
- **Foreground**: Single continuous gold pulse, then logo
- **Camera**: Static, centered, slow push-in

## Animation Sequence
1. **2:02-2:12**: Single gold pulse flickers
   - Pulse appears at center of frame
   - Slow, rhythmic pulsing (1 pulse per 2 seconds)
   - Pulse grows slightly with each cycle (breathing effect)
   - Opacity varies: 40% → 60% → 40%

2. **2:12-2:20**: Pulse transforms into AUREV ⚡ logo
   - Pulse expands outward
   - Logo materializes from within pulse
   - Logo glows at full brightness
   - Subtle particle effects around logo

3. **2:20-2:28**: Text overlay appears below logo
   - "The Unified Intelligence Civilization"
   - Fades in slowly (2 second fade)
   - Appears in Poppins Bold, #F8C948

4. **2:28-2:30**: Years appear below civilization text
   - "2025 – 2035"
   - Fades in after civilization text
   - Smaller font, Inter Regular

5. **2:30-2:35**: Final hold
   - Logo and text remain on screen
   - Subtle pulsing continues
   - Slow fade to black begins at 2:33

## Typography
- **Logo**: AUREV ⚡ (rendered as graphic, not text)
- **Main Text**: "The Unified Intelligence Civilization"
  - Font: Poppins Bold
  - Size: 42pt
  - Color: #F8C948
  - Position: Below logo, centered
  
- **Years**: "2025 – 2035"
  - Font: Inter Regular
  - Size: 24pt
  - Color: #EAEAEA
  - Position: Below main text, centered

## Color Palette
- Primary: #0A0A0A (black background)
- Logo/Pulse: #F8C948 (gold, full brightness)
- Main Text: #F8C948 (gold)
- Years Text: #EAEAEA (light gray)

## Sound Design Notes
- Pulse: Low-frequency heartbeat (40 Hz, subtle)
- Logo reveal: Orchestral swell (strings + brass)
- Text appearance: Gentle synth pad
- Final hold: Ambient pad fades to silence
- Fade to black: Complete silence (2-3 seconds)

## Duration
33 seconds (final frame extends to end of 3:45 runtime)
