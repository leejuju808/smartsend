# AUREV HQ Brand Guidelines

## Logo

### Primary Logo
**AUREV HQ ⚡**

The logo consists of:
- Text: "AUREV HQ" in bold, sans-serif
- Icon: Lightning bolt emoji (⚡) or custom SVG
- Tagline: "Where Intelligent Automation Meets Execution"

### Usage
- Use the full logo when space permits
- Icon-only version for favicons and social avatars
- Always maintain aspect ratio
- Never distort or rotate the lightning bolt

## Colors

### Primary Palette

**Gold/Amber (⚡ Lightning)**
- Hex: `#EAB308`
- RGB: `234, 179, 8`
- Usage: Primary CTA, highlights, accents

**Black (Background)**
- Hex: `#000000`
- RGB: `0, 0, 0`
- Usage: Primary background, contrast

**Charcoal (Secondary Background)**
- Hex: `#1F2937`
- RGB: `31, 41, 55`
- Usage: Cards, borders, secondary elements

**White (Text)**
- Hex: `#FFFFFF`
- RGB: `255, 255, 255`
- Usage: Primary text on dark backgrounds

### Accent Colors

**Gray Scale**
- Light Gray: `#9CA3AF` - Disabled states
- Mid Gray: `#6B7280` - Secondary text
- Dark Gray: `#4B5563` - Borders

## Typography

### Headings
- Font: Bold, modern sans-serif (Inter, System UI)
- Size: 2xl-5xl responsive
- Weight: 700-900 (Extra Bold)
- Color: White or Gold on dark backgrounds

### Body Text
- Font: System UI font stack
- Size: base (16px) with 1.5 line-height
- Weight: 400-500 (Regular to Medium)
- Color: White or Gray-400

### Accents
- Lightning bolt (⚡): Use generously for emphasis
- Bold tags: Use sparingly for key messages

## Tone & Voice

### Brand Voice
- **Professional yet approachable** - Speak to founders like peers
- **Data-driven** - Always lead with metrics
- **Execution-focused** - Emphasize results, not just features
- **Confident** - Bold claims backed by traction

### Messaging Pillars

1. **Automation First**
   - "AI should just handle it"
   - "Your business runs while you sleep"

2. **Unified Intelligence**
   - "One connected system"
   - "From cold email to CRM to AI agents"

3. **Modern Operators**
   - "Built for the next era"
   - "Automation for intelligent founders"

## Visual Style

### Photography
- Clean, modern, tech-focused
- Real operators and workspaces
- Natural lighting, minimal setup
- Black & gold color grading

### Illustrations
- Lightning bolt motifs throughout
- Clean, geometric patterns
- Minimal detail, maximum impact
- Gold accents on black backgrounds

### UI Screenshots
- Dark theme always (black background)
- Gold highlights on interactive elements
- Clean borders (charcoal)
- Lightning bolt favicons

## Usage Examples

### Marketing Materials
- Hero sections: Black background, gold CTA
- Feature cards: Charcoal background, white text
- CTAs: Gold buttons with black text
- Accents: Lightning bolts for emphasis

### Press Kit
- All assets should be dark mode
- Gold highlights for key metrics
- Lightning bolt watermarks
- Professional yet bold

### Social Media
- Profile: Black or charcoal background
- Posts: High contrast, gold accents
- Stories: Lightning bolt frames
- Videos: Black & gold intro animation

## Don'ts

❌ **Never:**
- Use light mode variants
- Change the gold color
- Remove the lightning bolt
- Use unprofessional language
- Water down the execution message
- Mix competing color schemes
- Use generic business imagery
