## The Problem We're Solving

**Tool Fragmentation** — Small businesses use 3+ disconnected tools for:
- Lead management (spreadsheets)
- Email campaigns (separate platforms)
- CRM and pipelines (standalone tools)
- AI automation (scattered solutions)

**Manual Work** — Founders waste 10+ hours/week gluing tools together

---

## Our Solution

### The AUREV HQ Ecosystem

Three interconnected apps sharing one intelligent layer:

**1. SmartSend** — Cold email automation
- Unified inbox for all replies
- AI-powered follow-ups
- Campaign builder with templates
- Reply detection and tone analysis

**2. OpsGrid** — CRM and workflow automation
- Contact management
- Deal pipeline tracking
- Workflow builder
- Task automation

**3. Agent Cloud** — Deployable AI agents
- Marketplace of pre-built agents
- Custom agent builder
- Cross-app trigger automation
- Autonomous task execution

### What Makes It Different
- **Native Integration:** All apps share one database and authentication
- **Intelligent Sync:** Data flows automatically between apps
- **Unified Billing:** One subscription, access to ecosystem
- **Cross-App Automation:** Events in one app trigger actions in others
