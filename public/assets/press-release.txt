SmartSend AI Launches the First Cold Email Operating System for SMBs

SmartSend AI, the new outreach automation platform built by AUREV Labs, enables small businesses to run complete AI-powered cold email systems — from lead import to automated replies — all in one place.

"Our mission is to make cold email as automated as social ads," says founder Julian Lee. "SmartSend runs 24/7, learning from replies and writing follow-ups automatically."

SmartSend is live at smartsendhq.com/demo.
