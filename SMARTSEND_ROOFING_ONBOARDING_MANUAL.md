## 🧱 SECTION 2 — ACCOUNT SETUP

### Step 1 — Business Settings

**Upload your logo**
- Go to: Settings → Business
- Click "Upload Logo"
- Use a high-quality image (PNG or JPG)

**Add business info**
- Business name
- Business address
- Business phone number
- Business email address

**Add office staff + assign roles**
- Click: Settings → Team
- Click "Add Team Member"
- Enter their name and email
- Assign role (Admin, Manager, Estimator, etc.)

**Add your crews**
- Click: Settings → Crews
- Click "Add Crew"
- Enter crew name (e.g., "Crew A", "Smith Team")
- Add crew members if needed

**Add your suppliers**
- Click: Settings → Suppliers
- Click "Add Supplier"
- Enter supplier name and contact info

### Step 2 — Email Connection

**Connect your sending inbox**

This lets SmartSend send outreach and follow-ups from your email.

- Go to: Settings → Email
- Click "Connect Email"
- Follow the prompts to connect your email account
- **Important:** Use the email address you want homeowners to see

### Step 3 — Payments

**Connect Stripe**

**⚠️ YOU MUST COMPLETE THIS STEP to collect deposits and payments.**

- Go to: Settings → Payments
- Click "Connect Stripe"
- Follow the Stripe connection process
- This takes about 5 minutes
- Once connected, you can collect payments immediately

### Step 4 — Document Templates (Optional for Day 1)

**Upload your templates:**

- **Contract** — Your standard roofing contract (PDF)
- **Estimate** — Your estimate template (PDF)
- **Change order template** — For scope changes (PDF)

**Where to upload:**
- Go to: Settings → Documents
- Click "Upload Template"
- Select the file
- Name it clearly (e.g., "Standard Contract 2025")

**Keep it simple.** You can add more templates later.

## 🧱 SECTION 3 — ADDING YOUR FIRST JOB

### ⚠️ THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP OF ONBOARDING

**Roofers MUST run a real job or they'll never feel SmartSend's power.**

### Step 1 — Add Homeowner

**Click:** Jobs → New Job

**Enter homeowner information:**
- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Address (full address)

**Click "Save"**

### Step 2 — Add Job Info

**Fill in the job details:**

- **Job name** — e.g., "Smith Roof Replacement"
- **Roof type** — Select from dropdown (Asphalt, Metal, Tile, etc.)
- **Crew** — Select the crew doing the work
- **Supplier** — Select the supplier providing materials
- **Notes** — Any special instructions or details

**Click "Save"**

### Step 3 — Add Documents

**Upload your documents:**

- **Estimate (PDF)** — Click "Upload Estimate"
- **Contract (PDF)** — Click "Upload Contract"
- **Photos (optional)** — Upload any photos you have

**Tip:** Drag and drop files directly into SmartSend.

### Step 4 — Send Estimate to Homeowner

**Click:** "Send for Signature"

SmartSend will:
- Create a professional email
- Send the estimate to the homeowner
- Track when they view it
- Track when they sign it

**You'll see status updates in real-time.**

### Step 5 — When Signed → Convert to Contract

**Once the homeowner signs the estimate:**

- SmartSend automatically updates the job status
- The estimate becomes a contract
- You can now schedule the job
- Payment collection is enabled

**That's it.** Your first job is live.

## 🧱 SECTION 4 — SCHEDULING & PRODUCTION SETUP

### Step 1 — Open the Calendar

**Click:** Production → Calendar

You'll see a calendar view of all your jobs.

### Step 2 — Drag Job Into a Day

**Drag your job** from the list into a day on the calendar.

**Set duration:**
- Click on the job
- Select duration (1 day, 2 days, 3 days, etc.)
- This helps SmartSend track progress

### Step 3 — Assign a Crew

**Assign a crew to the job:**

- Drag the crew onto the job, OR
- Click the job → Select crew from dropdown

**The crew will see this job in their Field App.**

### Step 4 — Sync With Supplier Delivery

**Add material ETA if known:**

- Click on the job
- Enter "Material Delivery Date"
- SmartSend will alert you if there are delays

### Step 5 — Watch SmartSend Alerts

**The system automatically detects:**

- ⚠️ **Weather issues** — Bad weather on scheduled days
- ⚠️ **Crew conflicts** — Same crew scheduled twice
- ⚠️ **Material delays** — Supplier delivery problems
- ⚠️ **Timeline risks** — Jobs running behind schedule

**This is where things start to feel "automatic."**

You'll get alerts in:
- Your dashboard
- Email notifications
- Mobile app (if installed)

## 🧱 SECTION 5 — DOCUMENTS & E-SIGN

**Upload your contract as a PDF:**

- Go to the job

- Click "Upload Contract"
- Select your PDF file

**SmartSend stores it securely.**

### Step 2 — Send to Homeowner

**Click:** "Send for Signature"

**One click.** That's it.

SmartSend:
- Creates a professional email
- Includes a secure signing link
- Tracks everything automatically

### Step 3 — Homeowner Signs

**The homeowner signs from their phone:**

- They receive an email
- Click the link
- Sign on their phone (or computer)
- Done in 30 seconds

**No app download required.**

### Step 4 — SmartSend Tracks Status

**You'll see real-time status:**

- ✅ **Viewed** — Homeowner opened the document
- ✅ **Signed** — Homeowner completed signature
- ⏳ **Changes needed** — Homeowner requested edits
- 📧 **Reminder sent** — Automatic reminder sent

**All visible in your dashboard.**

### Step 5 — Change Orders

**For scope changes:**

- Upload the change order PDF
- Click "Send for Signature"
- Homeowner signs
- Job updates automatically

**This alone replaces DocuSign + manual updates.**

No more:
- Printing contracts
- Driving to get signatures
- Manually updating spreadsheets
- Losing documents

## 🧱 SECTION 6 — PAYMENTS & INVOICING

### Step 1 — Send Deposit Request

**From any job:**

- Click "Payments"
- Click "Request Deposit"
- Enter the amount
- Click "Send"

**SmartSend creates a payment link automatically.**

The homeowner receives:
- A professional email
- A secure payment link
- Option to pay by card or ACH

### Step 2 — Homeowner Pays

**The homeowner clicks the link and pays:**

- Card payment (instant)
- ACH payment (1-2 business days)

**You don't need to do anything.**

### Step 3 — Track Payment Status

**You'll see real-time status:**

- 📧 **Sent** — Payment request sent
- 👁️ **Viewed** — Homeowner opened the link
- ✅ **Paid** — Payment received
- ⚠️ **Overdue** — Payment past due date

**All visible in your dashboard.**

### Step 4 — Send Final Invoice

**When the job completes:**

- Click "Payments"
- Click "Send Final Invoice"
- Enter the final amount
- Click "Send"

**SmartSend handles the rest.**

### Step 5 — Automations

**You can enable automatic reminders:**

- **Overdue reminders** — Auto-send if payment is late
- **Paid status notifications** — Alert you when paid
- **Scheduling triggers** — Auto-schedule after deposit received

**This creates clean cash flow.**

No more:
- Chasing payments
- Sending manual invoices
- Forgetting to follow up
- Payment delays

## 🧱 SECTION 7 — FIELD APP FOR CREWS

### Why Your Crews Need This

**Your crews MUST use SmartSend to upload:**

- Photos
- Notes
- Progress updates
- Check-in/check-out times

**This gives you REAL visibility into what's happening on-site.**

### Crew Flow (Simple)

**1. Crew opens the job**
- Crew member opens SmartSend Field App
- Finds the job for today
- Clicks on it

**2. Press Check In**
- Click "Check In" button
- Records start time
- Location is automatically tracked

**3. Upload photos as work progresses**
- Click "Add Photo"
- Take photo with phone camera
- Add notes if needed
- Click "Upload"

**4. Press Check Out**
- Click "Check Out" button
- Records end time
- Job status updates automatically

**That's it.** Simple.

### What You See

**As the owner, you see:**

- ✅ Crew checked in at 7:15 AM
- 📸 12 photos uploaded throughout the day
- 📝 Notes: "Found additional damage, discussed with homeowner"
- ✅ Crew checked out at 4:30 PM
- 📊 Job progress: 60% complete

**All in real-time.**

No more:
- "Where are you guys?"
