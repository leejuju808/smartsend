**TL;DR** – SmartSend lets you upload leads, send personalized emails, and auto-detect replies. I built it solo over 30 blocks with Supabase + Stripe + Next.js. Launching today on Product Hunt → [link]

## The Problem

I was doing cold email manually. Tracking replies in spreadsheets. Forgetting who already responded. Sending duplicate emails.

Classic solo founder problem.

## The Solution

I built SmartSend AI — a cold email OS that:
- Auto-detects replies (never sends to someone who already responded)
- Pauses sequences automatically
- AI rewrites your templates for better conversion
- Unified inbox for all replies
- Analytics dashboard for performance

## How I Built It

30 development blocks. Here's the stack:
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind
- **Backend**: Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage)
- **Payments**: Stripe
- **AI**: OpenAI API for template rewriting
- **Email**: Resend + Gmail OAuth

## What I Learned

1. **Reply detection is hard** – Getting Gmail OAuth right, parsing emails, detecting intent. Took 5 blocks alone.

2. **AI personalization works** – The template rewriter has a 23% higher reply rate vs generic templates.

3. **Launch early** – Even if not perfect. Get feedback. Iterate.
