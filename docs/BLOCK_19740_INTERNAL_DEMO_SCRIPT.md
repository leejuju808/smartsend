## Delivery Tips

### Tone
- **Confident** — You're showing something powerful
- **Fast-paced** — Keep energy high
- **Roofing-focused** — Speak their language
- **Enthusiastic** — Show genuine excitement

### Pacing
- **Don't rush** — But keep it moving
- **Pause for emphasis** — After key points
- **Match visuals** — Let actions complete before moving on

### Body Language (if live demo)
- **Point to screen** — Guide their attention
- **Use hand gestures** — Show "one tap" motion
- **Make eye contact** — Connect with audience
- **Smile** — Show confidence

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: "How does it know if a lead is hot?"
**A**: "AI analyzes the reply for keywords like 'estimate', 'leak', 'insurance claim' — roofing-specific signals. It gives each lead a score from 0-100. 80+ is hot."

### Q: "What if I want to see all replies, not just hot ones?"
**A**: "You can filter by intent — hot, warm, follow-up, or all. The default view shows everything sorted by priority."

### Q: "Does it work with my existing email?"
**A**: "Yes. SmartSend captures replies from any email address you use for campaigns. Everything appears in one unified inbox."

### Q: "What about mobile?"
**A**: "The Inbox works on mobile browsers now. A full mobile app is coming soon."

### Q: "How much does it cost?"
**A**: "SmartSend is in beta right now. Pricing will be announced at launch. But early beta users get special pricing."
