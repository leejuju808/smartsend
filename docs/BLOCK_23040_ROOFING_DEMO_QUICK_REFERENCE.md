# Block 23040 — SmartSend Roofing Demo Quick Reference

**One-page cheat sheet for sales calls**

## 🎯 Positioning (30 seconds)

**"SmartSend isn't a CRM. It's an AI engine that runs your revenue and your jobs for you.**

**Instead of hiring 3 extra office people, SmartSend handles the follow-up, the scheduling, the documents, the updates, and even payment reminders."**

---

## 📋 Demo Flow (8 Steps — Follow EXACTLY)

### 1. Lead Engine (The Hook)
- Show: Campaign builder → Personalized emails → Reply detection → Auto follow-up
- Say: *"This is how SmartSend fills your calendar without ads."*

### 2. Job Page (The Heart)
- Show: Schedule → Materials → Photos → Forecast → Profit → Messages
- Say: *"Everything your team touches happens in ONE place. No juggling JobNimbus + Excel + Group Texts + DocuSign + QuickBooks."*

### 3. Homeowner Portal (Trust Builder)
- Show: Photos → Updates → Documents → Messaging → Payment links
- Say: *"This is why customers stop blowing up your phone."*

### 4. Payments (Revenue Moment)
- Show: Collect deposit on call
- Say: *"You can collect deposits BEFORE a crew steps foot on the property."*

### 5. Automations (The Closer)
- Show: Overdue invoice → Crew arrival → Review request → Material delay
- Say: *"SmartSend handles the repetitive work so your team doesn't forget anything."*

### 6. AI Insights (The Jaw-Drop)
- Show: Margin risk → Schedule risk → Material delay → Daily briefing
- Say: *"This is like having a COO watching every job 24/7 and telling you what's going wrong before it hurts your wallet."*

### 7. Close
- Ask: *"Do you want SmartSend to run one of your jobs this week so you can see how much chaos it removes?"*
- **NOT:** "Do you want to buy?"
- **YES:** "Do you want it to run a job for you?"
