# SmartSend AI — 60-Second Demo Clip Script

## Production Notes

**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Recording Tool:** OBS Studio (or similar screen recorder)  
**Editing:** CapCut / Descript  
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30fps, MP4

---

## Script Breakdown

### 0:00-0:05 — Opening Title Card (5 seconds)

**Visual:**
- Black screen
- White text: "SmartSend AI"
- Subtitle: "The Cold Email OS That Runs Itself"
- Logo animation (fade in)

**Audio:**
- Upbeat tech music starts (lo-fi or electronic)

---

### 0:05-0:18 — AI Prospector Finding Leads (13 seconds)

**Visual:**
- Screen: Dashboard view
- Cursor hovers over "AI Prospector" button
- Click to open prospector interface
- Show: "Finding 50 qualified leads..." loading animation
- Results appear: List of leads with job titles, companies, LinkedIn profiles
- Highlight: "42 matches found" counter

**Voiceover:**
> "SmartSend's AI Prospector automatically finds and qualifies leads based on your criteria."

**Audio:**
- Music continues, subtle UI click sounds

---

### 0:18-0:35 — AI Writing & Sending Messages (17 seconds)

**Visual:**
- Switch to "Autopilot" tab
- Show: "Autopilot Active" badge (green, animated pulse)
- Show: Draft message preview — AI-generated personalized email
  - Subject: "Quick question about [Company Name]"
  - Body: Personalized content with {{first_name}}, {{company}} variables
- Show: "10,247 messages sent today" counter (updating live)
- Show: Multi-channel badges: Email ✉️, LinkedIn 💼, WhatsApp 📱

**Voiceover:**
> "Our AI writes personalized messages across Email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. Fully autonomous, with human-level quality."

**Audio:**
- Music continues, typing sound effect (subtle)

---

### 0:35-0:52 — AI Reply Handler Classifying & Booking (17 seconds)

**Visual:**
- Switch to "Inbox" view
- Show: Incoming reply message
- AI intent badge appears: "Interested - Wants Demo" (green)
- Show: "Book Meeting" button auto-highlighted
- Click "Book Meeting"
- Calendar picker appears, time slot selected
- Show: "Meeting booked: Tomorrow, 2 PM" confirmation

**Voiceover:**
> "When prospects reply, our AI classifies intent and can even book meetings automatically. No human required."

**Audio:**
- Music continues, success sound effect (subtle)

---

### 0:52-0:60 — Closing Card (8 seconds)

**Visual:**
- Analytics dashboard quick flash
  - Key metrics visible: "25% Reply Rate", "82% Retention"
- Fade to black
- White text: "Fully autonomous. Multi-channel. Now live."
- Logo + tagline: "SmartSend AI — SmartSend your outreach"
- Website URL: "smartsend.ai"

**Voiceover:**
> "SmartSend AI. The autonomous outreach OS that runs itself."

**Audio:**
- Music fades out, gentle closing note
