## 🎨 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Visual Effects
- **Film Grain:** 12 fps overlay, 0.3 opacity, monochrome
- **Gold Streak Transitions:** 0.8-second duration, ease-in-out
- **Particle Systems:** Gold particles (#F8C948), 60 fps physics, depth of field blur
- **Data Visualization:** Procedural gold lines, animated growth, depth-based transparency

### Audio Mix
- **Opening (0:00–0:48):** Orchestral → Electronic Hybrid (40% orchestra / 60% electronic)
- **Middle (0:48–2:12):** Ambient AI Tones → Orchestral Build (60% ambient / 40% orchestra)
- **Climax (2:12–3:18):** Full Orchestral + Choir (80% orchestra / 20% choir)
- **Epilogue (3:18–4:00):** Ambient → Silence (gradual fade)

### Typography Guidelines
- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold, 72–96pt, #F8C948 (gold), letter-spacing: 0.05em
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular, 24–36pt, #FFFFFF (white), line-height: 1.6
- **Metrics:** Poppins Bold, 48–60pt, #F8C948 (gold)
- **Fade-in Duration:** 0.5 seconds, ease-out
- **Fade-out Duration:** 0.8 seconds, ease-in

### Camera Movement
- **Slow Push-in:** 0.5–1.0 units/second
- **Orbital Rotation:** 0.3 radians/second
- **Dolly Forward:** 0.8 units/second
- **Crane Shot:** 0.4 units/second vertical

## 🎬 POST-PRODUCTION NOTES

### Color Grading
- **Shadows:** Deep blacks (#0A0A0A), crushed slightly (RGB: 10, 10, 10)
- **Midtones:** Neutral, slight cool tint (blue shift: -0.05)
- **Highlights:** Warm gold accent (#F8C948), highlight recovery: +0.15
- **Overall:** High contrast (1.2x), saturation boost on gold only (+0.3)

### Motion Graphics
- **Transitions:** Gold streak wipes (0.8s), cross-dissolves (0.5s)
- **Text Animations:** Fade-in with slight scale (1.0 → 1.05 → 1.0)
- **Data Visualizations:** Animated line growth (Bezier easing)

### Export Settings
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate:** 24 fps (cinematic standard)
- **Codec:** H.264 (MP4) for delivery, ProRes 422 HQ for master
- **Audio:** 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo
