## 🎨 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Palette
- **Primary Black:** #0A0A0A (RGB: 10, 10, 10)
- **Gold:** #F8C948 (RGB: 248, 201, 72)
- **White Accent:** #FFFFFF (RGB: 255, 255, 255)
- **Background Gradient (optional):** Radial gradient from #1A1A1A to #0A0A0A

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold
  - Sizes: 72pt–120pt
  - Color: #F8C948 (gold)
  - Letter-spacing: 0.05em
  - Line-height: 1.2

- **Body Text:** Inter Regular
  - Sizes: 24pt–36pt
  - Color: #FFFFFF (white)
  - Line-height: 1.6
  - Max-width: 80% of slide width

### Animation Guidelines
- **Fade-in Duration:** 0.5–0.8 seconds, ease-out
- **Fade-out Duration:** 0.3–0.5 seconds, ease-in
- **Slide Transitions:** Cross-dissolve (0.5s) or gold streak wipe (0.8s)
- **Text Appear:** Line-by-line fade with 0.3–0.5s delay between lines

### Visual Effects
- **Film Grain:** Optional 12 fps overlay, 0.2 opacity (subtle)
- **Gold Particles:** Low opacity (0.15–0.3), slow movement
- **Depth of Field:** Subtle blur on background elements (15–20px blur)
- **Glow Effects:** Gold glow on emblem/text (2–3px blur, 0.6 opacity)
