### SmartSend Company Policy (Institutional)

SmartSend is **company policy**, not a preference:

- **Outreach runs daily**: SmartSend outbound automation runs every day.
- **Follow-ups are mandatory**: follow-ups cannot be disabled.
- **Jobs must be logged**: if a job is won, it must exist as a `roofing_jobs` record in SmartSend.
- **Work happens in SmartSend**: inbox, lead status, follow-ups, and job progression live here.

This policy is enforced by the system (not by memory, habit, or a single person).

### What this changes (behavior)

- You do not ask “Should we run SmartSend?”
- You ask “Who is on duty for SmartSend today?”
- If SmartSend is paused manually, it will be restored automatically.
