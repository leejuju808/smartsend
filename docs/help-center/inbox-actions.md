# Actions You Can Take in the Inbox

The SmartSend Inbox gives you one-tap actions to handle every homeowner reply. Here's what you can do.

## Quick Actions

### 📞 Call Now
**When to use**: Hot leads, urgent repairs, homeowners asking for immediate help

**What happens**:
- Opens phone dialer with homeowner's number
- Logs the call in Activity Feed
- Creates a note if you add one

**Best for**: HOT leads (score 80+)

---

### 💰 Send Estimate
**When to use**: Homeowners asking for pricing, quotes, or estimates

**What happens**:
- Generates estimate link
- Sends email with estimate form
- Tracks when they view/submit estimate

**Best for**: WARM leads asking "How much?"

---

### ✅ Mark as Booked
**When to use**: Homeowner agrees to schedule estimate or book job

**What happens**:
- Marks lead as "Booked"
- Records estimated job value (optional)
- Stops follow-up emails
- Adds to your booked estimates count
- Tracks in Activity Feed

**Best for**: HOT leads ready to move forward

---

### 📋 Add Task
**When to use**: Need to follow up later, schedule callback, or remember something

**What happens**:
- Creates a task linked to this lead
- Sets due date and time
- Adds notes
- Shows in your Tasks list

**Best for**: WARM leads, follow-ups, reminders

---

### 🔗 Add to CRM
**When to use**: Want to sync lead to external CRM (Jobber, JobNimbus, etc.)

**What happens**:
- Exports lead data to your CRM
- Syncs contact info, notes, and status
- Keeps SmartSend and CRM in sync

**Best for**: Companies using external CRM systems

---

## When to Use Each Action

### Scenario 1: Hot Lead — Active Leak
**Reply**: "Yes, I have a leak in my kitchen. Can you come today?"

**Actions**:
1. **Call Now** — Call immediately
2. **Mark as Booked** — If they agree to estimate

---

### Scenario 2: Warm Lead — Pricing Question
**Reply**: "How much does a roof replacement cost?"

**Actions**:
1. **Send Estimate** — Get pricing in their hands
2. **Add Task** — Follow up in 3 days if no response

---

### Scenario 3: Follow-Up Needed
**Reply**: "Can you explain what you do?"

**Actions**:
1. **Add Task** — Respond with explanation
2. **Call Now** — If you want to explain over phone

---

### Scenario 4: Booked Estimate
**Reply**: "Yes, I'd like to schedule an estimate for next week."

**Actions**:
1. **Mark as Booked** — Record the booking
2. **Add Task** — Set reminder for estimate day
3. **Add to CRM** — If using external CRM

---

## Action Best Practices

### For HOT Leads (80-100 score)
- **Call Now** is your best friend
- Respond within 1 hour for 80% win rate
- **Mark as Booked** as soon as they agree

### For WARM Leads (60-79 score)
- **Send Estimate** to get pricing in their hands
- **Add Task** to follow up in 3-5 days
- Don't let them go cold

### For Follow-Up Needed
- **Add Task** to remember to respond
- **Call Now** if you prefer phone conversations
- Respond within 24 hours

### For Booked Estimates
- **Mark as Booked** immediately
- Enter estimated job value for tracking
- **Add Task** for estimate day reminder

## Activity Feed

All actions appear in the Activity Feed:
- "Called Sarah M."
- "Sent estimate to John D."
- "Marked as booked — $15,000 estimated value"
- "Task created: Follow up in 3 days"

This gives you a complete timeline of every interaction.
