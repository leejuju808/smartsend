# Understanding Lead Scores

Lead scores help you focus on the homeowners who are most likely to book a job. Here's how they work.

## What is a Lead Score?

A lead score is a number from 0-100 that represents how likely a homeowner is to book a roofing job. Higher scores = higher priority.

## Score Ranges

### 🔥 HOT (80-100)
**Book estimate NOW**

These homeowners are ready to move:
- Asking for estimates or quotes
- Wanting inspections or appointments
- Reporting active leaks or damage
- Mentioning insurance claims
- Asking "Can you come this week?"

**Action**: Call immediately. These are money-in-hand leads.

### 🟡 WARM (60-79)
**Follow up this week**

These homeowners are interested but not urgent:
- Asking pricing questions
- Considering roof work
- "Maybe next month"
- General interest, no urgency

**Action**: Send estimate link or schedule follow-up call.

### 🔵 COLD (40-59)
**Low priority**

These homeowners replied but aren't ready:
- "Not right now"
- "Maybe later"
- General questions
- Low engagement

**Action**: Add to follow-up sequence or mark for later.

### ⚫ NOT INTERESTED (0-39)
**Stop sending**

These homeowners declined:
- "Not interested"
- "Already handled"
- Unsubscribed
- Spam or invalid

**Action**: SmartSend automatically stops sending emails.

## What Makes a Lead "Hot"?

### Reply Signals (+25 to +75)
- "Yes" → +70
- "What's the price?" → +55
- "Can you come this week?" → +80 (auto-HOT)
- "Is this covered by insurance?" → +65
- General reply → +25

### Intent Classification (+35 to +80)
- HOT intent → +80
- WARM intent → +50
- FOLLOW-UP → +35
- NOT_INTERESTED → -20

### Storm Risk (+20 to +40)
- Hail zone → +30
- Windstorm → +20
- Recent heavy rain → +15

### Insurance Indicators (+30 to +60)
Keywords: adjuster, claim, inspection, payout

### Repair Signals (+20 to +50)
Mentions: leaks, missing shingles, flashing, vents, skylights

### Replacement Signals (+40 to +70)
Keywords: full roof, replacement, shingles worn, roof is old

### Engagement Behavior (+10 to +30)
- Multiple opens → +10
- Consistent engagement → +15
- Clicked link → +30

## What Should You Do Next?

### For HOT Leads (80-100)
1. **Call immediately** — Use the "Call Now" button
2. **Book estimate** — Use "Mark as Booked" if they're ready
3. **Send estimate link** — If they want pricing first

### For WARM Leads (60-79)
1. **Send estimate link** — Get pricing in their hands
2. **Schedule follow-up** — Add a task to call next week
3. **Answer questions** — Respond to their inquiries

### For COLD Leads (40-59)
1. **Add to follow-up sequence** — Let SmartSend nurture them
2. **Mark for later** — Review monthly
3. **No immediate action needed**

### For NOT INTERESTED (0-39)
- SmartSend automatically stops sending
- No action needed

## Tips

- **Focus on HOT leads first** — They have the highest conversion rate
- **Respond quickly** — HOT leads have 80% win rate if responded within 1 hour
- **Don't ignore WARM leads** — They often convert with proper follow-up
- **Let COLD leads nurture** — They may become HOT later
