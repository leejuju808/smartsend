# How the Inbox Works

The SmartSend Owner Inbox is your unified command center for every homeowner reply. Here's how it works.

## Overview

When a homeowner replies to any SmartSend email campaign, their message automatically appears in your Inbox — no matter which campaign it came from, no matter which email address they replied to.

## The Inbox Flow

### 1. Reply Arrives
- Homeowner clicks "Reply" on any SmartSend email
- Their message is captured instantly
- AI analyzes the reply for intent and urgency

### 2. AI Classification
- **Hot Lead**: Wants estimate, appointment, or immediate repair
- **Warm Lead**: Interested but not urgent (pricing questions, considering)
- **Follow-Up Needed**: Needs manual response (questions, unclear intent)
- **Not Interested**: Declined or unsubscribed

### 3. Lead Scoring
Every reply gets a lead score (0-100):
- **80-100**: HOT — Book estimate NOW
- **60-79**: WARM — Follow up this week
- **40-59**: COLD — Low priority
- **0-39**: Not interested or spam

### 4. Inbox Display
- Replies appear in chronological order
- Hot leads are highlighted with red badges
- Lead score and intent tag are visible
- AI summary shows what the homeowner wants

## Key Features

### Unified View
All replies from all campaigns appear in one place. No more switching between Gmail tabs or checking multiple inboxes.

### Real-Time Updates
New replies appear instantly. No refresh needed. The Inbox updates automatically.

### Smart Filtering
Filter by:
- Intent (Hot, Warm, Follow-Up, Not Interested)
- Campaign
- Date range
- Lead score

### Activity Timeline
See the complete conversation history:
- Outbound emails you sent
- Homeowner replies
- Actions taken (calls, estimates, bookings)
- Tasks created
- Notes added

## What Happens Next?

1. **Hot Lead**: Click "Call Now" or "Mark as Booked"
2. **Warm Lead**: Send estimate link or schedule follow-up
3. **Follow-Up Needed**: Review and respond manually
4. **Not Interested**: SmartSend automatically stops sending

## Getting Started

1. Go to **Owner Inbox** in your SmartSend dashboard
2. Review new replies (they're sorted by priority)
3. Take action using the one-tap buttons
4. Track everything in the Activity Feed
