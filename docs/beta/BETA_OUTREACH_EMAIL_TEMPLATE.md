# Beta Outreach Email Template

Copy/paste template for reaching out to potential beta testers (3-5 roofing companies).

## Subject Line

```
Quick question about filling your roofing schedule
```

## Email Body

```
Hey — quick question…

I built a system that helps roofing companies book more homeowner jobs automatically using AI-powered outreach.

It fills your schedule with real people needing roof work — without spending on ads.

I'm testing it with 3–5 roofing companies privately before I open it up.

Would you want in?

It comes with:
- Full AI follow-up system
- Hot lead alerts to your phone
- Roofing templates built in
- Reply detection
- Personalized homeowner emails
- Everything automated

If you want a spot, let me know — I can show you a quick demo.

— Julian
```

## Personalization Tips

- Use their first name if you know it
- Mention their company name: "I noticed [Company Name] does roofing work in [City]..."
- Reference local area if relevant: "I'm focusing on roofing companies in [City/Region]..."

## Follow-Up (If No Response After 3-5 Days)

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about filling your roofing schedule

```
Hey — just following up on my last message.

I'm still looking for 2 more roofing companies to test SmartSend before launch.

The beta includes:
- 30 days free
- $99/mo locked-in price forever (normally $149/mo)
- Private onboarding with me
- Access before everyone else

Only 2 spots left. Interested?

— Julian
```

## What to Include When They Respond

1. **Quick qualification questions:**
   - How many employees? (looking for 3-20)
   - Do you have an office person or estimator? (need someone to handle leads)
   - What's your biggest challenge getting leads? (validate pain point)

2. **Schedule the 20-minute demo:**
   - Use Calendly or similar
   - Keep it short: "20 minutes to show you how it works"

3. **Set expectations:**
   - "This is a private beta — only 5 companies total"
   - "You'll get 30 days free, then $99/mo locked forever"
   - "I'll personally help you set it up"

## Red Flags (Don't Onboard These)

- ❌ "Let me try it for free first" (not committed)
- ❌ Tech-savvy marketers (not your target)
- ❌ Agencies managing multiple companies (too complex)
- ❌ Companies with complex CRMs already (won't see value)
- ❌ Companies that won't take action (waste of time)

## Green Flags (Perfect Beta Testers)

- ✅ Owner-led companies (decision maker involved)
- ✅ 3-20 employees (right size)
- ✅ Has office person/estimator (can handle leads)
- ✅ Says "I just need more jobs" (clear pain point)
- ✅ No complex systems (will see immediate value)
- ✅ Takes action quickly (will actually use it)

---

**Remember:** You're not selling yet. You're inviting them into an exclusive beta. Keep it simple, low-pressure, and focused on their benefit.
