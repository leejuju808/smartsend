## ✅ G. Beta Rules (For You)

### Rule #1:

**You do NOT onboard all 5 at once.**

Do it one at a time.

- Onboard #1 → stabilize
- Onboard #2 → stabilize
- Onboard #3 → stabilize
- Then add #4 and #5

**Why?**

Because early beta testers break your system in unexpected ways.

You want space to react.

### Rule #2:

**Fix EVERY bug instantly.**

Anything that affects:
- Sending
- Replies
- Routing
- Alerts
- Billing

**MUST be fixed same day.**

### Rule #3:

**You collect testimonials quietly.**

You do NOT post them yet.  
You store them for your December 2026 Domination Launch.
