# Demo Video Script — SmartSend AI (90 seconds)

## Production Notes
- **Tools**: OBS + CapCut
- **Branding**: Use black-and-gold lightning overlay for consistency
- **Duration**: 90 seconds
- **Export**: 1080p, 30fps for social platforms

---

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1 — Hook (0-10 seconds)
**Visual**: Lightning bolt animation → SmartSend AI logo fade in
**Audio**: Upbeat, energetic background music starts

**Script**:
> "Cold email shouldn't feel cold. Meet SmartSend AI ⚡ — the all-in-one cold email OS."

---

### Scene 2 — Problem (10-25 seconds)
**Visual**: Split screen showing:
- Left: Person manually checking emails
- Right: Excel sheet with reply tracking
- Clock ticking animation

**Script**:
> "Manual outreach kills time — tracking replies, sharing campaigns, rewriting messages…"

---

### Scene 3 — Solution (25-55 seconds)
**Visual**: Screen recording of SmartSend dashboard with voiceover highlights

**Script** (spoken over screen recording):
> "Show dashboard:
> 
> Upload CSV → validate leads
> 
> Auto-sends queueing through your mailboxes
> 
> Replies tab lights up with human detections
> 
> Smart Template Rewriter generates 3 versions instantly"

**Screen Recording Cues**:
- **0:25-0:32**: Click "Import CSV" → show validation success
- **0:32-0:40**: Show queue dashboard with emails "queued" → "sent"
- **0:40-0:48**: Replies tab showing notifications (red badge, "New Reply Detected")
- **0:48-0:55**: Open template rewriter, show 3 generated variants side-by-side

---

### Scene 4 — Results (55-75 seconds)
**Visual**: Animated stats/graphs overlaying dashboard:
- "40% faster replies" (green up arrow)
- "3× more booked calls" (calendar icon + multiplier)
- "Zero busywork" (checkmark icon)

**Script**:
> "Teams see 40% faster replies, 3× more booked calls, and zero busywork."

---

### Scene 5 — CTA (75-90 seconds)
**Visual**: Full screen CTA card with SmartSend logo and lightning bolt

**Script**:
> "Start your free plan at smartsendhq.com — and let AI send for you."

**Text Overlay**:
- Large: "smartsendhq.com"
- Small: "Start Free →"
- Branding: Lightning bolt ⚡ animation
